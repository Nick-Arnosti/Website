A Model World

I often find myself translating real-world scenarios into mathematical models. This helps to make claims precise, and clarify the assumptions on which they rest. On this blog, I share some of these perspectives with you!

My hope for this blog is to spark conversations with those around me. I thrive on exchange of ideas, so please add comments to the posts! Your comments will help refine my thinking, and will also make my life better.

Finally, the blog features different types of posts. While I hope that many are accessible to a broad audience, some require a fair degree of technical sophistication. If you reach a post that wasn’t meant for you, just find another!
