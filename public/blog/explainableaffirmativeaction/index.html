Examples of Selection Rules

  • Diversity Visa Lottery: Starting at the top of the priority list, accept applicants unless doing so violates a country quota, regional quota, or the global quota.
  • NYC Affordable Housing Developer Screening
    • Consider applicants eligible for disability preference in log order until 7% of units have been allocated.
    • Consider applicants eligible for community preference in log order until 50% of units filled with people from community district.
    • Consider applicants eligible for municipal employee preference in log order until 5% of units filled with municipal employees.
    • Consider remaining applicants in log order until all units filled.
  • Accept the top applicant, then accept the top remaining female applicant.
  • Accept the top female applicant, then accept the top remaining applicant.

Axioms of Explainability

A selection rule is

  • Monotonic if increasing priority of \(i\) (fixing others’ priority) weakly helps \(i\).
  • Lower Invariant if \(i\)’s outcome does not depend on the ranking of individuals with lower priority than \(i\).
  • Non-Bossy if any change to \(i\)’s priority that does not affect \(i\)’s outcome also does not affect others’ outcomes.

Definition 1 A selection rule \(\varphi\) is monotonic if for any \(i \in \mathcal{I}\), and any priority orders \(\succ, \succ'\) such that (i) \(\succ_{-i}~=~\succ'_{-i}\) and (ii) the priority of \(i\) is higher in \(\succ'\) than in \(\succ\), then \[i \in \varphi(\succ) \Rightarrow i \in \varphi(\succ').\]

Has also been called “respecting unambiguous improvements.”

Definition 2 A selection rule \(\varphi\) is lower invariant if for any applicant \(i \in \mathcal{I}\), and any priority orders \(\succ, \succ'\) such that \(\succ_{-\{j:i\succ j\}}~=~\succ'_{-\{j:i\succ' j\}}\), we have \[\{i\} \cap \varphi(\succ) = \{i\} \cap \varphi(\succ'). \]

Definition 3 A selection rule \(\varphi\) is non-bossy if for any applicant \(i \in \mathcal{I}\), and any priority orders \(\succ, \succ'\) such that \(\succ_{-i}~=~\succ'_{-i}\), \[\{i\} \cap \varphi(\succ) = \{i\} \cap \varphi(\succ') \Rightarrow \varphi(\succ) = \varphi(\succ').\]

Outcome-Based Selection Rules

Theorem 1 For any collection \(\mathcal{F}\subseteq 2^{\mathcal{I}}\), the selection rule \(OB_{\mathcal{F}}\) is monotone, non-bossy and lower invariant.

If \(\varphi\) is a selection rule that is monotone, non-bossy and lower invariant, then there exists \(\mathcal{F}\subseteq 2^{\mathcal{I}}\) such that \(\varphi(\succ) = OB_{\mathcal{F}}(\succ)\) for every priority order \(\succ\).
