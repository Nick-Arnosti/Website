Four Rivers Lottery and Permit Reservation System:

Separate applications for private and commercial groups. Roughly 3-5 groups of each type per day.

Serial dictatorship w/max list length of four. Can apply once per river, but can only accept one river. Do they enforce this during drawing, or during acceptance phase? If former, what order are lotteries drawn in? If latter, why not just do one big SD (say, list 16 options across rivers -- this tells you what they will take, perhaps minimizing double wins, and allows for intensity of preference, as you can list more than 4 for some rivers and fewer than four for others).

Can see how many listed each date first, second, third, fourth. All very oversubscribed. Can also see where applicants are from!
