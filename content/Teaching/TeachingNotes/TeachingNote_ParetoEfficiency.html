Learning Objectives

  • Students will be able to define Pareto efficiency and Rank Efficiency.
  • Students will be able to determine whether or not an allocation is Pareto efficient.
  • Students will be able to use the serial dictatorship and first preferences first algorithms to find Pareto efficient allocations.

Key Concepts and Algorithms

  • Pareto Efficiency
  • Serial Dictatorship
  • First Preferences First

Lesson Plan and Commentary

Mistakes and Misconceptions

  • Students often conflate Pareto efficiency with “Pair Efficiency” (the absence of pairs of people who want to swap items). Simple examples can be used to illustrate that Pair Efficiency does not imply Pareto Efficiency.
